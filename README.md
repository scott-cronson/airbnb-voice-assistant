# airbnb-voice-assistant
Exploratory voice experience for searching and booking on Airbnb

This is a thought experiment and PM proof-of-concept exploring how Airbnb could be redesigned voice-first — targeting high-frequency business travelers (sales reps, consultants) who book repeatedly while driving or in transit, where voice offers the strongest advantage over manual input. The interaction model follows a natural 3–5 turn conversational flow: state destination and dates, review preference-ranked options, confirm, and book. Built as a rapid prototype in a few days, it uses mock data and localStorage in place of a live backend — intentional shortcuts to demonstrate the core booking experience and surface product assumptions worth validating before any real investment.
